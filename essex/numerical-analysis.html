Who Develops Diabetes? A Statistical Analysis of the Pima Indian Diabetes Database

Recorded presentation and R analysis

This statistical analysis covers 768 women of Pima heritage, using eight clinical predictors. It documents two data decisions that shape every result: treating impossible zero values as missing, and recognising that the outcome is defined by glucose. The analysis then moves from descriptive statistics and inferential tests to linear and logistic regression, including a BMI by age interaction. All analysis was carried out in R.
